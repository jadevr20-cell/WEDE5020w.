                     Focus on sustainable gardening, food production, environmental education and community participation
                     The purpose of this website is to introduce All Green Foundation to the community and provide information about its activities, projects and environmental goals


Website Goals and Objectives: Introduce All Green Foundation
	                      Explain the purpose and vision of the project
	                      Show the vegetables and herbs grown in the garden

	                      Provide information about sustainable gardening

	                      Provide contact information

Timeline and Milestones:   Phase	                         Activities	                                   Time
                        1. Planning	        Identify site, partners, volunteers and funding	                 Months 1–2
                        2. SitePreparation	Clear area, improve soil and create garden beds	                 Months 2–3
                        3. Water System	        Install water-saving and rainwater collection systems	         Months 3
                        4. Planting	        Plant vegetables, herbs and suitable indigenous plants	         Months 4
                        5. Training	        Begin practical gardening workshops	                         Months 4–12
                        6. Maintenance	        Water, weed, compost, monitor plants and manage pests	         Months 4–12
                        7. Harvesting	        Harvest and distribute/share produce	                         Months 5–12
                        8. Evaluation	        Measure participation, harvests and environmental benefits	 Months 6–12


Part 1 Details: All Green Foundation will provide more than a place to grow food. It will create a practical learning environment where people can develop gardening skills, work together and learn how to protect natural resources. The project combines food production, education, environmental responsibility and community development into one sustainable initiative.
                The website will provide an important communication platform where people can learn about the project, participate in activities and discover ways  to practice sustainable gardening.
                All Green Foundation garden will communicate its vision and encourage more people to become involved.
